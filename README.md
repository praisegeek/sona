# Sona
### *The Telegram for Music*

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg)](LICENSE)
[![Network: P2P](https://img.shields.io/badge/Network-WebRTC%20//%20IPFS-green.svg)]()
[![Platform: Zero-Cost](https://img.shields.io/badge/Hosting-%240%20Server%20Cost-orange.svg)]()

**Sona is an open-source, unstoppable music protocol. By replacing costly centralized streaming servers with peer-to-peer (P2P) networking and permanent decentralized storage, Sona runs at zero infrastructure cost—delivering 100% free, ad-free music in every language, forever.**

</div>

---

## 🚀 Core Features

- **🚫 Zero Ads & Zero Paywalls:** Completely free for listeners, with no corporate ad interruptions or subscription paywalls.
- **🌐 P2P & Decentralized Architecture:** Audio streams directly between users via P2P nodes and permanent storage, meaning **$0 platform server costs**.
- **⚡ Shazam-Like Discovery:** Built-in acoustic fingerprinting to instantly identify tracks playing around you and drop them into community threads.
- **🎛️ The Open Music Studio:** A native creative suite allowing anyone to pull track stems, remix songs, and utilize integrated lip-sync technology to create viral audio-visual shorts.
- **💸 Direct Track Tipping:** No middlemen or label cuts. Fans tip artists directly via instant micro-transactions.
- **🌍 Borderless & Multilingual:** Designed from the ground up to surface and scale music across all languages and borders.
